Honestly

Words first. Photos second.

Honestly is a free, ad-free iOS dating app. Every profile leads with a written, on-the-record answer to one of a small set of honest prompts — and you only see, and are seen by, people whose intentions (serious, casual, friendship, still figuring it out) overlap with yours.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no third-party tracking. Sign in with Apple only.
