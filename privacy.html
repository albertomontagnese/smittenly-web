Privacy Policy

Effective May 2026

Honest Dating ("the app") is built and operated by Alberto Montagnese. This page describes exactly what is and isn't collected, and how it's used. The app's only authentication method is Sign in with Apple, and the app's backend runs on Google Firebase (Firestore + Cloud Storage + Firebase Authentication).

Data we collect

  • Apple-issued user identifier. When you Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app a stable opaque user id (a "uid"). We store it as the primary key of your profile in our database.
  • Profile data you enter. Your first name, date of birth (used to derive your age and to enforce the 18+ gate — only your age, never your full birthday, is shown to other users), the gender you identify as, the genders you'd like to see, your chosen prompt and prompt answer, the freeform "region" string (e.g. "Brooklyn, NY"), your optional bio, your accepted age range, and your "what I'm here for" (intentions) selection.
  • Profile photos. The photos you upload are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage under your uid. Other signed-in users can see them, but they are not publicly listed or indexed.
  • Swipes. When you swipe left or right on someone, we store the swipe under your uid so we know not to show that person again, and so we can detect mutual likes.
  • Matches and chat messages. When two people both swipe right on each other, a "match" record is created. Messages sent inside that match are stored alongside it. Only the two participants can read them; server-side security rules enforce this.
  • Reports. If you report another user, we store your report (your uid, the reported uid, and the reason you picked). We use this to triage abuse.
  • Block list. If you block someone, we store both sides of the block on your profile so the deck and matches list can filter accordingly.
  • Crash + diagnostic logs. Apple sends us aggregate crash reports if you've enabled "Share with Developers" in iOS Settings → Privacy. These contain no personal data.

Data we don't collect

  • We don't ask for your email address, phone number, or any government identifier.
  • We don't ask for or store your race, ethnicity, religion, weight, sexual orientation (beyond the genders you indicate you'd like to see), or any other protected characteristic.
  • We don't request or use location services. Your "region" is whatever free text you typed in the app.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or analytics.
  • We don't run third-party tracking SDKs, attribution networks, or ad networks. The only third-party SDK in the app is Google Firebase, which we use as our database / storage / auth backend.
  • The "Smart Filter" natural-language search runs entirely on your device. The text you type into it is not sent anywhere.

Third parties

  • Apple handles authentication via Sign in with Apple. Apple does not see your activity inside the app.
  • Google Firebase (a service of Google LLC) hosts our database (Firestore), file storage (Cloud Storage), and auth bridge (Firebase Authentication). Firebase processes your profile data, photos, swipes, matches, messages, and reports as our data processor under a standard agreement.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit anything. Profile → edit your prompt, bio, photos, region, age range, intention, or "show me" preferences at any time.
  • Block / unmatch / report. Available from every chat header.
  • Delete your account. Profile → "Delete account" removes your profile document and your photos from our backend and signs you out. Messages you sent inside still-existing matches may persist for the other participant until they unmatch you; this is documented in the in-app delete confirmation.

Data retention

Profile data lives until you delete your account. Reports are kept for as long as needed to triage and act on abuse. Crash logs delivered through Apple's TestFlight / App Store Connect channel are retained according to Apple's policies.

Children

Honest Dating is 18+. The onboarding flow blocks any birth date that makes the user younger than 18, and the security rules treat profiles with `age < 18` as not viewable in the deck regardless of client behavior. The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
