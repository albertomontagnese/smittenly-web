Common questions

I'm not seeing anyone in the deck.

Honest Dating only shows you people whose intention overlaps with yours (for example, "serious" only matches "serious"; "casual" matches "casual" or "still figuring it out"), and whose age range overlaps with yours mutually. If your filters are tight, the deck can be empty until more people sign up. Try widening your age range in Profile, or relaxing your intention to "still figuring it out."

Why isn't there a race / weight / body-type filter?

That's intentional. We don't ask for or store these and we don't let people filter on them. We think structuring matchmaking around protected characteristics tends to make dating worse rather than better, and the research and the press cycles around it back that up. The Smart Filter on the Discover tab will deliberately ignore those terms if you type them.

How do I edit my prompt or my intention?

Profile tab → "Honest reason" (or whatever heading your current prompt shows) → Edit. To change the prompt question itself, tap one of the other options before saving. Same for "What I'm here for" — tap a different intention to change it.

How does the Smart Filter work?

You type a description in plain English. We extract age, intention, and keyword filters from the text using a small on-device parser — nothing you type leaves your device. The chip at the top of the Discover tab shows you exactly which filters were applied so there's no mystery.

How do I block or report someone?

Open the chat with that person, tap the "…" menu in the top right, and pick Block or Report. Both also unmatch you. Reports go to a moderation queue we review within 24 hours.

How do I delete my data?

Profile tab → "Delete account." This removes your profile and photos from our backend immediately and signs you out. Messages you sent inside still-existing matches may persist for the other participant until they unmatch you — this is described in the in-app confirmation.
